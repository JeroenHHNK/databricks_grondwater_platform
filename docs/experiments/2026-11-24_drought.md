# 2026-11-24 — Drought: an indicator or a soil-moisture bucket as an extra stress

Branch `research/accepted-models`. Plan: [PLAN_drought.md](PLAN_drought.md). Notebook: `pastas/notebooks/exploration/droogte.ipynb`.

## 1. Question
In the July 2026 drought (Beemster radar P 5 mm, Makkink 114 mm) many models stayed too high.

**The example** is BE_0072_BUKR_PB2 (filter −7 to −8 m NAP, pick `linear_dexp_lake_baro_radar`):
- observed: −0.38 m from June to the end of July, then +0.25 m within days of rain;
- simulated: −0.11 m.

**The user's question:** does a soil-moisture bucket or a drought indicator help? It must stay computable for the IMPREX return periods.

**On "autoregressive":** Pastas' deterministic part is a linear convolution, not an AR model. The AR part is only the noise model, and our fits have none. The limit is linearity: one gain per mm in any season.

## 2. Set-up (decided before the results)
**Indicators** (`pastas/research/accepted/drought.py`) come from the model's own P and E only, so they are rebuilt per IMPREX member:

| part | what |
|---|---|
| `def<D0>` | KNMI potential precipitation deficit from 1 April, above D0 (0, 50, 100, 150 mm) |
| `roll<days>` | E − P over 30, 60, 90 days, dry side |
| `bkt<Smax>l<lp>` | root-zone bucket deficit; Smax 50, 100, 150, 250 mm; lp 0.5, 0.7 |

**The model term:** `StressModel(indicator, Exponential, up=None)`, so the gain sign is free.
- It goes on top of the pipeline's extended set: lake + pressure, on Berkhout (`wl1+baro`) or radar (`radar+wl1+baro`).
- **Fitted nested.** First the model without the term, then the drought model starting from its parameters, with the drought gain started at either sign; the lower SSE wins.
  - The holdout and leave-out refits do the same on their own training data.
  - Without this the optimiser sometimes ended worse than the model without the term.

**Scores per model** (`run_variants.py`):
- RMSE 1 Jul–15 Aug 2026;
- the same with **Jun–Sep 2026 left out of the fit** (the out-of-sample test);
- the recovery RMSE 16 Aug–30 Sep;
- spring 2025;
- the error on the drop from June to the end of July;
- the holdout R² (the last 30 %, which contains the drought).

**Decision rule:**
- a global setting, not per series;
- better out of sample (sign test);
- passed not lower (McNemar);
- holdout median of the picks not lower;
- no runaway.

**Stages:**
1. All 15 settings on `linear_{exp,gamma,dexp}` + `wl1+baro`.
2. The two best settings on all six types (incl. fourparam and flex) and both forcings.

The run is on the frozen 89 validated series.

## 3. Results
**Like for like** (`results/drought_paired.csv`): each drought model against the same series, type and forcing without it. Negative = better.

| indicator | models | ΔAIC median | Δ drought RMSE, left out (cm) | better / worse | p | Δ holdout R² |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **bkt150l7** | 1062 | −118 | **−0.75** | 640 / 398 | <0.0001 | **+0.070** |
| bkt100l7 | 1062 | −110 | −0.70 | 658 / 380 | <0.0001 | +0.050 |
| roll60 | 267 | −88 | −0.70 | 161 / 99 | 0.0001 | −0.004 |
| bkt100l5 | 267 | −86 | −0.55 | 177 / 83 | <0.0001 | +0.044 |
| def0 | 267 | −84 | +0.12 | 124 / 136 | 0.50 | −0.018 |
| def100 | 267 | −101 | **+0.77** | 106 / 153 | 0.004 | −0.030 |
| def150 | 267 | −109 | **+0.81** | 105 / 155 | 0.002 | −0.077 |

**The bucket with lp 0.7 generalises.** The KNMI deficit with a threshold fits the one drought in-sample best (lowest AIC) and is worse out of sample: overfitting on a two-year record.

**With the selection and IMPREX** (`results/drought_extremes.csv`; `select_models` with the runaway rule, 16 members):

| scenario | passed (of 89) | gained / lost | runaway picks | picks with the bucket | holdout R² median |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| reference (extended set) | 60 | – | 0 | 0 | 0.483 |
| + bkt100l7 | 64 | 4 / 0 | 0 | 61 | 0.483 |
| **+ bkt150l7** | **65** | 5 / 0 (p = 0.06) | 0 | 61 | 0.483 |

- **The reference reproduces the pipeline:** 60 of the frozen 89, as in the 2026-11-24 export.
- **The five gains** pass by `flex_gamma@radar+wl1+baro+bkt150l7` (4) and `linear_dexp@wl1+baro+bkt150l7`.
- **Return periods:** the dry T100 of a passed pick sits a median 4.2–4.7 cm above the record low, against 4.5 cm without the bucket. In about 20 % of picks it is deeper than the record low, in both scenarios.
  - The record low contains the noise (residual RMSE about 5 cm), and the simulated extremes do not, so a gap of this size is expected.
  - The bucket deepens dry levels by mm to a few cm (example series: dry T100 −1.7 cm).

**Where it does not help** (`results/drought_series.csv`):
- **8 strong drought misfits** (drought RMSE above 1.5 × the overall RMSE): 7 in the Beemster, 6 too high.
- **The deep Beemster filters** (−6 m NAP or deeper): 5 of 13 are misfits, with a median drop error of 5.2 cm (4.2 cm with the bucket). For the Purmer's deep filters it is 0.9 cm.
- **The example:** the drop error goes from 0.27 to 0.24 m.

**At BE0072+3:**
- the crest and toe PB2 (−7 m) drop together in a **sawtooth**: about 0.2 m down, then back within days, several times, with no rain;
- the shallow PB1 (−3.4 m) declines smoothly by 0.13 m;
- the 4 Aug low (−4.764 m) was confirmed real by the user.

## 4. Conclusions
1. **A soil-moisture bucket as an extra stress works** and fits the IMPREX pipeline: the indicator is rebuilt from each member's P and E, with no held mean. Recommended setting: **`bkt150l7`** (Smax 150 mm, lp 0.7), one setting for every series. It gives +5 passed, none lost, a better drought fit out of sample, and a higher holdout R².
2. **The KNMI deficit with a threshold overfits.** It is not to be used.
3. **The big misses are not a soil-moisture problem.** The deep Beemster aquifer drops in steps and recovers within days, which looks like **pumping** (sprinkling from groundwater or another abstraction). No P/E indicator can reproduce that. It needs abstraction data (HHNK/province permits, pumping records, sprinkling bans).
4. **KNMI'23 scenarios are not needed for this.** IMPREX has P and E, which is all the bucket uses. KNMI'23 stays an option for a climate sensitivity (transform the members, recompute the indicator).

## 5. Mistakes and corrections
- **The first smoke fit with the term ended worse than without it** (R² 0.566 vs 0.647). The drought gain starts at +1/std, the wrong sign. Fixed with the nested fit and two signs.
- **A selection-only comparison hid the effect** (61 → 62–64 in stage 1, picks' drought RMSE flat). The like-for-like comparison is the right lens. Stage 1 also offered the indicator on only three types.
- **The pick moves with the bucket.** For BE_0072_BUKR_PB2 it goes from `linear_dexp@radar+wl1+baro` to `linear_exp@wl1+baro+bkt150l7` (on Berkhout). So compare a series like for like (`drought_paired.csv`, the notebook), not pick against pick.
